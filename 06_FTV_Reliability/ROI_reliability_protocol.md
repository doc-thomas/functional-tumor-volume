# ROI-derived FTV measurement reliability

Physician 4, with more than 15 years of breast imaging experience, independently delineated ROIs in a random 30% sample from each training and test set. ICCs were calculated for the resulting FTV measurements. Values were interpreted as poor (0.00–0.20), fair (0.21–0.40), moderate (0.41–0.60), good (0.61–0.80), or excellent (0.81–1.00).

Table S20 summarizes 440 candidate FTV definitions per subtype, subset, and early window. Medians and interquartile ranges describe variation across definitions; the bars are not confidence intervals for an individual ICC. FTV_ICC_estimates.csv provides the available estimates. TNBC_ROI_measurements and TNBC_ICC_estimates contain the available supporting measurement and per-definition result tables; the result tables identify ICC1 where supplied. Original numerical results are preserved.

The ROI agreement assessment is distinct from paired manual-versus-automated FTV agreement and repeat-scan reliability, which were not evaluated. The supplied window-a test measurement tables contain different subset sizes and should not be paired solely by row order.
